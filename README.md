# ScholarMind

AI Research and Knowledge Management Project

Overview

ScholarMind is an AI-based research and knowledge management project designed to organize, connect, and evolve scientific knowledge.

It aims to empower researchers with an AI companion that remembers, connects, and accelerates scientific discovery.

ScholarMind is not designed simply to create a smarter AI. Instead, it is designed to help humans think better, remember better, and collaborate more effectively in research.

Goals

ScholarMind aims to:

- Organize research knowledge
- Connect related scientific information
- Support research analysis
- Maintain research memory
- Enable knowledge evolution
- Help researchers find relevant information faster
- Connect knowledge through a Knowledge Graph
- Preserve the research journey through Research Memory
- Support cross-disciplinary collaboration
- Accelerate the emergence of scientific innovation

Core Concepts

Knowledge Graph

ScholarMind connects scientific information, concepts, researchers, methods, datasets, and publications through a structured Knowledge Graph.

Research Memory

ScholarMind preserves the history and evolution of research, allowing researchers to maintain context, previous findings, experiments, decisions, and insights.

Knowledge Evolution

ScholarMind is designed to support the continuous evolution of scientific knowledge by connecting new findings with existing knowledge and research history.
