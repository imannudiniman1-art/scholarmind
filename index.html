ScholarMind AI Research and Knowledge Management ScholarMind is an AI-based research and knowledge management system designed to organize, connect, and evolve scientific knowledge. Research Intelligence ScholarMind combines: - Artificial Intelligence - Research Memory - Knowledge Graph - Scientific Literature - Research Workflow Project ScholarMind aims to provide researchers with an intelligent research companion that can help organize knowledge, connect scientific information, and support research development.
