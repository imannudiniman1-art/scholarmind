# ScholarMind Concept

## Overview

ScholarMind is an AI-based research and knowledge management
project designed to organize, connect, and evolve scientific knowledge.

## Core Concepts

- AI-assisted research
- Research memory
- Knowledge graph
- Knowledge evolution
- Scientific knowledge management

## Purpose

ScholarMind aims to help researchers organize research information,
connect related knowledge, and support the development of new insights.

## Project Status

Current stage: Early prototype / research concept.
