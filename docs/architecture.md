# ScholarMind Architecture

## Overview

ScholarMind is designed as a modular AI research and knowledge
management system.

## Main Components

### 1. Research Input

Research papers, datasets, notes, and scientific information
are collected as research inputs.

### 2. Knowledge Processing

AI-assisted processes analyze, classify, and organize research
information.

### 3. Research Memory

Important research information and relationships are maintained
as structured knowledge for future use.

### 4. Knowledge Graph

Related concepts, papers, methods, datasets, and findings can
be connected through a knowledge graph.

### 5. Knowledge Evolution

New research information can be connected with existing knowledge
to support continuous development and discovery.

## Initial Architecture

Research Input
    ↓
Knowledge Processing
    ↓
Research Memory
    ↓
Knowledge Graph
    ↓
Knowledge Evolution
    ↓
Research Support

## Development Principle

ScholarMind is designed as a modular system so that individual
components can be improved independently over time.
